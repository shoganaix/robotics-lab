# 🚗 Logic-Gate Controlled Car

[![English](README.md)](#) [![Español](README.es.md)](README.es.md)

Remote-controlled car whose **direction and steering are implemented with digital logic gates** (no microcontroller involved). The chassis and the steering mechanism were fully **modeled by me in 3D**.

## Status

Done — working prototype with real photos.

## Overview

- **Hardware-based control logic** — direction (forward/backward/turn) decided by combinational logic gates
- **Custom steering mechanism** — rack-and-pinion style steering, designed and modeled by me
- **Chassis fully modeled by me** — 3D-printed frame integrating all the electronics
- **Mechanical + electronic fusion** — the mechanics and the logic layout were designed together
