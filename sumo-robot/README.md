# 🤼 Sumo Robot with Suction System

[![English](README.md)](#) [![Español](README.es.md)](README.es.md)

Sumo robot designed for **sumo-style robotic competitions**, built around a **custom 3D-printed chassis** and a **ground suction system** that increases traction and resistance to being pushed out of the ring.

## Status

Done — competition-ready mechanical design.

## Repository Layout

```text
sumo-robot/

    └── Carchasis.max    # Chassis 3D model (3ds Max source)
```

## Overview

- **Custom 3D chassis** — the entire frame was designed by me in **3ds Max** (`hardware/Carchasis.max`)
- **Ground suction system** — increases grip on the ring for better pushing force
- **Optimized weight distribution** — low center of gravity for stability in combat
- **Robust mechanical structure** — designed for autonomous combat orientation
- **Embedded control electronics** — to be documented alongside the assembly

## Hardware

Open `hardware/Carchasis.max` with Autodesk 3ds Max to edit/export the chassis. STL exports for 3D printing and the assembly photos will be added to this folder.
